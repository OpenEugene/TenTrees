# Program Reporting

Centre staff (10 Trees Admin or Project Manager) generate reports and export data to track program outcomes and report to funders. Reporting is a high-priority, staff-only function; mentors and growers do not have access to it.

## Reports

- **Tree Survival Rate** — filtered by village and date range (for example, Orpen Gate Village for 1–31 October 2025); shows total trees planted, total alive, and the survival rate percentage, which is the alive count as a share of the planted count (e.g. 500 planted, 455 alive, 91% survival).
- **Permaculture Practices** — per-village percentages for each practice (making compost, collecting water, using greywater, no chemical fertilizers, no pesticides) plus a callout of areas needing improvement (the lowest-scoring practice). For example, if Orpen Gate Village scores 85% compost, 90% water collection, 75% greywater, 95% no chemical fertilizers and 92% no pesticides, the report reads "Areas needing improvement: Using greywater".
- **Monthly Village Report** — for a chosen month and village (e.g. November 2025, Orpen Gate Village), includes tree survival rate and trend, new enrollment count for the month, completed assessment count, permaculture compliance percentages, and identified areas for improvement.
- **Home Visits** — for a date range, shows total home visits per staff member (either filtered to one staff member — e.g. Joel with 25 visits in March 2025 — or one row per staff member showing their count for that period); results are exportable, as this report is produced for funders. Visit recording is described in [Garden Assessment](/Specifications/Garden-Assessment).
- **Cohort Comparison** — lists cohorts with household counts (for example, Orpen Gate Village 2023 with 153 households, Orpen Gate Village 2024 with 57, and Roebuck 1 2026 with 55), with survival and compliance metrics filterable by cohort. See [Cohort Management](/Specifications/Cohort-Management).

## Data export

- **Export to Excel** downloads a `.xlsx` containing all currently filtered records (for example, one village over a preset range such as "Last 30 days"), with columns matching the data grid.
- **Export to CSV** downloads a `.csv` of the filtered data (such as assessment data) compatible with Excel import.

Exports are language-neutral regardless of the language a form was submitted in — see [Data Entry Language Independence](/Specifications/Localization/Data-Entry-Language-Independence).
