# Class Attendance

Centre staff track permaculture training class attendance for growers so the program knows who is eligible to receive trees. This is a medium-priority workflow.

## Standard class sequence

The standard permaculture education sequence is five classes:

1. Tree Selection
2. Tree Planting
3. Water Management
4. Soil Fertility
5. General Permaculture Practices

Class sequencing can vary by cohort. For the **Roebuck 2026 cohort**, classes 1 and 2 (tree selection and planting) are front-loaded before winter so trees can go in the ground within two weeks; classes 3–5 are delivered after trees are planted. Regardless of order, growers need **5 of 5** classes to be eligible for trees.

## Recording attendance

Attendance is recorded against a specific training class (for example, "PE Training Session 3" or "Tree Selection"), and that class may belong to a cohort such as Roebuck 1 2026. Marking a grower present at a class increments their attendance count by one and records which specific class they attended (e.g. "Tree Selection"). A grower's record shows progress as "N of 5 classes completed" — marking Mary Nkuna present at her third class takes her count to 3 and her record reads "3 of 5 classes completed".

## Eligibility

- A grower with 5 of 5 classes (for example, Mary Nkuna once she has attended all five) shows status **"Eligible for trees"**.
- A grower with fewer shows the remaining count and is not marked eligible — Grace Sithole, having attended 3 of 5, shows "2 classes remaining".
- Front-loaded classes do not bring eligibility forward. In a cohort such as Roebuck 1 2026, where Tree Selection and Tree Planting are taught first so trees can be planted early, a grower who has attended only those two still needs the remaining three: Sipho Dlamini shows "2 of 5 classes completed", is not marked "Eligible for trees", and a note reads "Remaining classes: Water Management, Soil Fertility, General Permaculture Practices". Trees may be planted before eligibility is reached; eligibility is about completing the full programme.

## Class–cohort association

Training classes can be linked to cohorts:

- From the training edit screen, an admin selects an active cohort (e.g. Roebuck 1 2026) from the add dropdown; the cohort appears as a tag on the class (e.g. "PE Training Session 1") and the link is also visible from the cohort edit screen.
- The training sessions list shows each class's linked cohorts as badges on its row — a class linked to both Roebuck 1 2026 and Orpen Gate Village 2024 shows both names.
- Removing a cohort tag from a class requires an inline confirmation; once confirmed, the cohort is no longer linked to that class.

Full cohort lifecycle and tag behaviour is documented in [Cohort Management](/Specifications/Cohort-Management).
